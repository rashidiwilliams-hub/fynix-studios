Shipping & Delivery

What to expect when your order is on its way.

Delivery Areas & Times

We currently deliver across Georgetown and surrounding areas, daily from 9:00 AM to 10:00 PM.

Delivery Fee

A flat delivery fee of G$800 applies to every order, calculated automatically at checkout.

Estimated Delivery Time

Most orders arrive within 35–45 minutes of being placed, depending on distance and order volume.

Order Tracking

You'll receive a confirmation with your order number once your order is placed. For questions on an order in progress, call us at +592 226-8990.

Returns & Refunds

Order Issues

Because our food is made fresh to order, we don't accept returns — but if something arrives incorrect, missing, or not up to standard, contact us within 24 hours and we'll make it right with a replacement or refund.

How to Request a Refund

Email hello@tastehaven.gy or call +592 226-8990 with your order number and a description of the issue.
