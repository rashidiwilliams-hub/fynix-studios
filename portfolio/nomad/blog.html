Style Notes From Nomad

Fit guides, fabric stories, and the thinking behind what we make — rooted in Guyana, made for everywhere you go.

September 8, 2026 · Style Guide

5 Ways To Style Cargo Pants This Season

Cargo pants stopped being just a streetwear staple a while back — now they're the easiest way to dress up or down depending on what you pair them with. Tuck in a fitted top and add heeled boots for something that reads more evening than utility. Or keep it loose with a cropped hoodie and low-top sneakers for the everyday version. The pocket details do a lot of the work, so let them show — avoid layering a long coat that hides the leg entirely. For cooler evenings, our cream oversized puffer plays surprisingly well against the wide leg, balancing volume on top with volume on the bottom instead of fighting it. And if you want one pair that does it all, go with a neutral shade first — black or olive — before adding color into the rotation.

August 22, 2026 · Travel

Building A Wardrobe That Travels

A capsule wardrobe isn't about owning less for the sake of it — it's about making sure everything you pack actually gets worn. Start with three neutral basics you'd happily wear three days in a row, then build out from there: one layering piece for temperature swings, one pair of shoes that works from morning to night, and one item that feels a little more "you" for when you want to stand out. That's usually enough for a week away, regardless of where you're headed. The name Nomad wasn't an accident — everything we make is built to hold up in a suitcase as well as it does in a closet, without needing an iron or a second thought.

August 3, 2026 · Behind The Brand

Why We Choose Premium Cotton — And Stick With It

It would be cheaper to cut corners on fabric. It would also mean everything we make starts pilling and losing shape within a few washes, which isn't what "everyday essentials" is supposed to mean. We use heavier-weight, premium cotton blends specifically because they're built for actual daily wear — not just how something looks on a hanger. It costs more up front, and we've stayed with it anyway, because a t-shirt that still fits the same after a year is worth more than one that's replaced every season. That's the whole idea behind Nomad: fewer pieces, held to a higher standard, worn more.
