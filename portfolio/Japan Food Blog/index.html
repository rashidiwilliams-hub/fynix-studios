About Guyana Bites

Guyana Bites is a running notebook of the meals worth crossing town for — the street corners, market stalls, and unmarked kitchens that make up Georgetown's real food scene. No star ratings, no sponsored posts. Just the dishes we keep going back for, written up by people who live here and eat here every day.

We're most interested in the food that doesn't have a sign: the pholourie cart that only shows up after 6pm, the bakery behind the market that sells out by nine. If you know a spot, tell us — that's usually how the best stories start.
