Reserve A Table

Sourced from regional farms and fisheries within a hundred miles whenever possible. Every plate begins over live coals for depth and char, then finished with restraint — simple technique, honest ingredients, no excess.

Ask your server for our current by-the-glass pairing — our list changes with the season alongside the menu. A bottle from our natural wine selection is a favorite match for anything off the fire.

Please inform your server of any allergies before ordering. Our kitchen handles nuts, shellfish, and dairy, and we're happy to accommodate most dietary needs with advance notice.
