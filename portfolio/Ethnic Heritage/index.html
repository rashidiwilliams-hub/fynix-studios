Our Story

One Table, Many Homelands

Ethnic Heritage began with a simple idea: that a shared table is the fastest way to understand a culture. Our kitchen brings together recipes passed down through families from West Africa to the Andes, from the Levant to Southeast Asia — each dish cooked the way it's cooked at home.

We work with small growers and spice importers who care as much about origin as we do, and we train every cook in the techniques specific to each region we serve — no shortcuts, no fusion for fusion's sake.
