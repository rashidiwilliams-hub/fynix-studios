Brief

We start with a conversation.

Tell us what the business does, who it sells to and what you want the project to achieve. WhatsApp, a call or in person, whichever is easiest.

You
Share your goals, examples you like and a rough budget.

Ask questions and look at what you already have: site, logo, accounts.

SHEET 02 / 07SCOPE & QUOTENEXT: DESIGN
02

Scope & quote

Everything agreed before work starts.

We turn the brief into a clear list of pages, features and deliverables, with one price and a plan for when each part is paid. A deposit is required before production begins, with the remaining balance payable according to the agreed project milestones. Our Christmas package requires a 50% deposit. Payment arrangements for other projects are outlined in your quotation. We accept cash, MMG, bank transfers and cheques.

You
Review the scope and price, and tell us what to change.
We
Write the scope, set the price and map out the milestones.
You get
A written quote and a milestone plan.

SHEET 05 / 07REVIEWNEXT: LAUNCH
05

Review

Your turn to push on it.

Go through the preview page by page and tell us everything that needs to change. We fix it, then check speed and the basics that help people find you. Three rounds of revisions are included, within the agreed project scope.

You
Go through the preview and list every change.
We
Make the revisions and fix any issues.
You get
The revised version, ready for your sign-off.
SHEET 06 / 07LAUNCHNEXT: SUPPORT
06

Launch

We put it live.

We connect your domain, publish the site and check every page, form and link on the live address. Eligible website packages include the first year of standard hosting. Renewal costs and additional infrastructure requirements are outlined in your quotation. Your domain remains registered in your name, and your business content and customer data remain yours. Website usage rights, source-code ownership and transfer arrangements are outlined in your project agreement.

You
Approve the launch and share domain access if you have one.
We
Publish, redirect old pages and test everything live.
You get
A live site, plus your files and logins handed over.

What we need from you

Standard starting documents

  • A signed quote and the deposit
  • Your logo and brand assets: colours and fonts, if you have them
  • Text and photos for each page, or tell us to write and source them
  • A list of your products or services, with prices
  • Domain and hosting logins, if you already have them
  • Business details: name, address, phone, WhatsApp and email
  • Your social media links
  • The name of the person who approves each stage
Questions

Frequently asked

How does payment work?

A deposit is required before production begins, with the remaining balance payable according to the agreed project milestones. Our Christmas package requires a 50% deposit. Payment arrangements for other projects are outlined in your quotation. We accept cash, MMG, bank transfers and cheques.

How long does a project take?

Establish Your Presence and Fresh Coat of Paint take about 3 days each, a standard Open 24/7 storefront about 6 weeks, and Automate It about 3 days. Zero to Launch depends on the business, and we confirm the timeline in your quote. Timelines begin once the deposit, content and approvals are in.

What if I need advanced e-commerce features?

Online payments, customer accounts and inventory integrations are available according to project requirements. They are scoped and quoted separately from a standard storefront.

How many revisions do I get?

Three rounds of revisions are included, within the agreed project scope. Work beyond the agreed scope is quoted separately.

Who owns the website, the domain and my content?

Your domain, business content and customer data remain yours. Website usage rights, source-code ownership and transfer arrangements are outlined in your project agreement.

What does hosting cost?

Eligible website packages include the first year of standard hosting. Renewal costs and additional infrastructure requirements are outlined in your quotation.

What support do I get after launch?

Support is outlined in your quotation. Fixing faults in the work we deliver is separate from paid support arrangements for content updates, enhancements and ongoing maintenance.

What do you need from me to start?

A signed quote and deposit, your brand assets, your content and your business details. The full list is above.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. With Fresh Coat of Paint we refresh the look of an existing site, including Wix and WordPress, without a full rebuild.
