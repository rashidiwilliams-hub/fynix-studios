The brief

A trusted name that needed a front door

Caribbean Chemicals Guyana has supported farmers since 1991 and now carries more than 120 products across 10 regions. Farmers, agronomists and distributors all rely on the company, and each needs something different from it.

They needed a website that presents the full range clearly, shares practical crop advice, and speaks to farmers in both English and Portuguese.

What we built
  • Product catalogue by categoryInsecticides, fertilizers, herbicides, fungicides, seeds, tools and equipment, and spare parts, so farmers can find the right product fast.
  • English and PortugueseA language switch in the header, so visitors can read the site in Portuguese as well as English.
  • Downloadable crop guidesPractical guides for crops such as papaya, watermelon, lettuce and celery, available as PDFs straight from the homepage.
  • Their story and approachA company timeline from 1966 to today, and a four-step look at how every product goes from a farmer's problem to a tested solution.
  • Farmer testimonialsWords from farmers in Canal #2, Parika Back Dam and West Coast Berbice, placed where buyers are deciding.
  • Brochures, calendar and distributorsThe supporting material a supplier needs in one place, with phone numbers and WhatsApp one tap away.
