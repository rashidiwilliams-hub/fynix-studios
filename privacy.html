Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy covers the Fynix Studios website and mobile app (together, the "Service"). Fynix Studios is a design and development studio based in Georgetown, Guyana.

What we collect

We only collect information you choose to send us. If you submit a quote request in the app, we receive your name, your email address or phone number, the service you are interested in, and the project details you type. If you contact us by WhatsApp, phone or email, we receive whatever you send in that conversation.

How we use it

We use this information only to reply to your request, prepare a quote and follow up about your project. We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties for their own marketing.

What we do not collect

The app does not require an account, does not track your location, does not use advertising identifiers, and does not include analytics or advertising SDKs.

Where it is stored

Quote requests are stored in a database hosted by Supabase. Access is limited to Fynix Studios. The website loads fonts and scripts from third-party content delivery networks, which may see your IP address when your device requests those files.

Retention and deletion

We keep quote requests for as long as needed to handle your inquiry and any resulting project. You can ask us at any time to delete the information you sent by emailing the address below. We will delete it within 30 days.

Children

The Service is aimed at businesses and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Continued use of the Service after a change means you accept the updated policy.
